Dashboard Workflow Guide

Step 1: Upload Your Card PDF & Base Prompt

In your LLM web app (Gemini, ChatGPT, or Claude), upload your full 20-page Brisnet or DRF PDF once. Select your strategy profile on Tab 1, click "Copy Prompt 1", and paste it in. The AI will isolate and analyze that specific race.

Step 2: Live Race-Day Odds & Track Scratches

Before post time, click the "DRF Live Odds Board" link inside Step 2. Select all text on the DRF odds page (Ctrl+A / Cmd+A), copy it, and paste the raw text directly into the box. Click "Generate Live Update Prompt" and paste that into your LLM to update probabilities and spot overlays.

Step 3: Multi-Model Committee Synthesis

If running multiple AI models (e.g., Claude + ChatGPT + Grok), paste each model's summary output into the respective text boxes in Step 3. Click "Compile Master Consensus Prompt" to calculate average consensus scores and generate final wager tickets.

💡 Pro-Tip: Tab Navigation

Switch between Race tabs freely. All typed live odds, notes, and generated prompts remain saved in background memory for every race on the card.
